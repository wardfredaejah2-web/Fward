Upcoming Activities

Our activities give members a chance to try something new and learn from each other.

These are planned activities. Dates, times, and locations will be announced.

Web Development Workshop

Learn how HTML organizes a page and how CSS changes its appearance. Practice adding navigation and making a page work on a phone.

What to bring

A laptop if available, your webpage files, and questions.

Monthly Coding Challenges

Work through a small coding problem at your own pace. Compare approaches and ask other members for help.

Who can join

Beginners and experienced students are welcome.

Guest Speaker Sessions

Hear from technology professionals or alumni about their experiences and career paths.

How to prepare

Think of questions about college, technology careers, or building job skills.

Team Hackathons

Work with a small team to plan and build a project that solves a problem. Share what you made with the club.

Skills to practice

Problem solving, teamwork, and explaining your ideas.

Computer Hardware Workshops

Learn about common computer parts and basic troubleshooting. Follow instructions before handling equipment.

Skills to practice

Identifying components and working safely.
