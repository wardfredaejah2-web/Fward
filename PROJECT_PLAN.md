# Tech Connect Website Plan
Fredaejah Ward — CIS 207

## Purpose and Audience
Help prospective student members learn about Tech Connect. Beginners and students interested in technology are the main audience.

## Content and Organization
One club page includes the welcome, mission, membership benefits, upcoming activities, member resources, and joining steps, in that order. The two outside links lead to MDN and freeCodeCamp.

## Wireframe
The layout in wireframes.html shows a blue header, white content area with clearly labeled sections, and a footer.

## HTML and CSS
Use semantic header, main, section, and footer elements. Include headings, paragraphs, a horizontal rule, an unordered list, an ordered list, and two external links. Link styles.css for colors, typography, spacing, list styles, and hover effects.

## Responsive Design and Future Work
At smaller screen sizes, remove outside margins and reduce heading sizes and padding. Continue improving this same project later in the semester. Add confirmed meeting details when available.
