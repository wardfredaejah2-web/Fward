How to Join

If you are interested in technology and willing to learn, Tech Connect is a place to get started.

Getting Started

  1. Check the activities page for club announcements.
  2. Attend an orientation or introductory meeting when details are posted.
  3. Tell the club organizer what you want to learn.
  4. Join a workshop or team project and get involved.

Contact Us

Official contact information and a membership form will be added when available.

This is a class project. Membership registration is not open on this website yet.

Common Questions

Do I need coding experience?

No. Beginners can ask questions and learn alongside other members.

What activities can I join?

Planned activities include web development workshops, coding challenges, guest speakers, hackathons, and hardware workshops.

What should I bring?

A laptop can help during workshops if you have one. Meeting announcements will explain what is needed.

Where are the meeting details?

Check the activities page. Dates and locations will be posted when confirmed.
